QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

What is Winter Arc? +

Winter Arc is a 90-day personal consistency challenge. You choose daily activities, complete them and track your progress.

Can I do it with a friend? +

Yes. Send your friend a request using their username. After they accept, both of you can view each other's progress.

Can my friend change my activities? +

No. Your friend has a read-only view of your dashboard. Only you can add, edit, remove or mark your own activities.

Can I edit a previous day? +

Yes. Your dashboard includes a history/calendar view where you can open previous days and update your own progress.

What happens if I forget a day? +

Nothing is automatically deleted. The day simply remains incomplete until you update it. Your dashboard will show the actual progress you recorded.

Is my data visible to everyone? +

No. Your dashboard is private. Only an accepted accountability partner can view your Winter Arc progress, and they cannot edit it.

What happens when the 90 days end? +

Your historical data remains available so you can review your consistency and compare your final results with your starting point.
