FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the Winter Arc? +

Winter Arc is a 90-day personal consistency challenge that starts on the day you choose and runs for 90 days.

Can I create my own activities? +

Yes. You can add activities, edit their names and targets, or remove activities from your routine.

Can I edit previous days? +

Yes. The calendar lets you select previous days and update the activities you completed on those dates.

Can my friend change my data? +

No. Your friend's dashboard is intended to be read-only. Your own data remains under your account.

How do I sign in? +

Winter Arc uses Google Sign-In. There is no email-and-password login form.
