Privacy Policy & Terms

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

What we store. Track stores the activities you create, their categories and targets, daily done or not-done status, deep-work minutes and notes, and your theme choice.

Where it is stored. If you are not signed in, your data stays only in this browser and does not leave your device. If you sign in with Google, your data is stored only in the cloud (Google Firebase Firestore), linked to your Google account. Your data is never kept in both places.

AI assistant. When you use the assistant, your message, plus the names, categories and today's status of your activities, is sent through a relay we run (Cloudflare Workers) to Groq, which generates the reply. The chat is kept only in the open tab and is cleared when you reload. Nothing in your data changes unless you confirm it.

Sharing. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Service providers: Google (sign-in and storage), Groq (AI replies), Cloudflare (assistant relay).

Deleting your data. Use Export, then Danger zone, then Delete all data. For signed-in users this removes your cloud data and the device copy. Deleting your Google account is separate and is managed by Google.

Your choices. You can export your data at any time.

Contact. Questions: github.com/mohakdev1220.
